Sip Note

A local tasting journal for the glass you just had

Coming soon to Google Play

A dram of whisky, a bottle shared at dinner, a beer somewhere far from home. The taste is vivid that night and, a week later, collapses into "it was good". Sip Note keeps that glass — with a photo, a score, and the place you had it.

Features

Log it

  • Category (whiskey, wine, beer, sake, cocktail), half-point scores, tags, memo, price
  • Multiple photos per note
  • A score control that fills the glass as you drag

Places

  • Attach a place and it lands on your map as a pin
  • Save the ones you want to visit to a wishlist

Pairings

  • Rate how well a food matched, in four steps
  • Look back by drink or by dish

Look back

  • Category mix, monthly score trend, places and cities visited
  • Your most-used tags and your best match
  • 15 badges that open up as notes add up

Stays on this device

No account, no server, no ads. Everything lives in a database on your phone. When you want a backup, export it yourself as JSON, CSV, or a zip that carries your photos — and restore from that file whenever you need to.
